# Attribution and source terms

REQ source material should be attributed to the Australian Government
Department of Industry, Science and Resources, Resources and Energy Quarterly.
The June 2026 workbooks state CC BY 4.0, except for the Commonwealth Coat of
Arms, trademarks, logos and third-party material.

`req_attribution()` exposes this separation. The MIT licence applies to package
code only and does not grant rights to excluded source material.

Committed fixtures are reduced, derived and synthetic. They reproduce
structures and failure modes but contain no official workbook values, logos,
Coat of Arms or third-party material.
