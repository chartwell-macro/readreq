# Attribution and source terms

Source terms are edition-specific. The June 2026 material requests attribution
to the Department of Industry, Science and Resources, Commonwealth of
Australia, Resources and Energy Quarterly June 2026, and states CC BY 4.0
subject to exclusions including the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, trademarks,
logos, third-party content, and other material noted by the source. Historical
editions can use different terms, including older Australian Creative Commons
versions. Start from the exact edition page linked in the release catalogue or
the official [Resources and Energy Quarterly collection](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/resources-and-energy-quarterly).

`req_attribution()` returns package guidance, not a legal determination for
every source asset. **Package code and package-authored synthetic fixtures:
MIT, copyright Chartwell Macro.** That package licence does not apply to
runtime-downloaded official material or other upstream content.

Committed fixtures are reduced, derived and synthetic. They reproduce
structures and failure modes but contain no official workbook values, logos,
Coat of Arms or third-party material.

The README and Getting Started article also display one derived chart generated
through `read_req_history()` and `read_req_forecasts()`. It combines the June
2026 quarterly iron ore export-volume history with forecast paths from the
March 2024, March 2025 and June 2026 REQ editions. Only the rendered PNG is
committed; the workbooks and extracted data are not. The selected tables credit
ABS Cat. No. 5368.0, the Department of Industry, Science and Resources, and
other named sources. Each source publication and ABS website material state CC
BY 4.0 subject to named exclusions; source-stated exclusions and third-party
rights continue to apply. The chart contains no source logo, Coat of Arms or
decorative publication content and is not covered by the package's MIT
licence. See `inst/COPYRIGHTS` for the exact provenance statement.
