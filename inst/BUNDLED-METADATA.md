# Bundled metadata and fixtures

The files under `catalogue/`, `config/`, and `schema/` are package-authored or
curated metadata used to define `readreq`'s release, parsing, selection, and
output contracts. The release catalogue records factual edition metadata and
links to official landing pages and assets; it does not bundle official
workbooks, reports, branding, or observations. **Package code and
package-authored metadata: MIT, copyright Chartwell Macro.**

The two workbooks under `extdata/` are reduced synthetic fixtures. Their
specific provenance and licensing statement is in `extdata/README.md`.

Files downloaded at runtime are not bundled package assets and are not
relicensed by `readreq`. They remain subject to the terms and exclusions
attached to the relevant source edition; see `req_attribution()` and the
official release page before reuse.
